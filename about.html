About JustGo

A simple, private messaging platform built for college students — to share notes, assignments, files, and stay in touch with classmates across every course and year.

← Back to JustGo

💡 Why JustGo exists

Many students need to share assignments, ask doubts, and stay connected with classmates. JustGo solves that with a lightweight web app that works in any browser — no install, no phone number, no email required.

"Just open the website, log in with your username, and everything you need is right there."

⏱️ Files auto-delete after 24 hours

To keep JustGo free for everyone, every file you send is automatically deleted 24 hours after it's uploaded. Text messages stay, but files don't.

⚠️
Please use files responsibly. If you need to keep a PDF, image, or assignment longer than 24 hours, download it to your device before it expires. Each file shows a countdown badge like "Deletes in 18h" so you're never caught off guard.

🔐 Security & privacy

JustGo is built directly on High-encrypted security and a secure backend. Here's how your data is protected:

Passwords

  • Passwords are never stored in plain text. They are hashed with bcrypt — a one-way cryptographic function. Not even the developers can read them.
  • You control your password. There is no admin override.

Messages

  • Row Level Security (RLS) is enforced at the database level. Each user can only read messages in conversations they are a member of.
  • No user can read messages from a conversation they aren't part of — not even by manually changing the URL or guessing IDs.
  • Messages are stored in a PostgreSQL database with per-row access rules.

Files

  • Files are uploaded to a private bucket. They are only accessible via short-lived signed URLs (valid for 1 hour).
  • No permanent public URLs exist. Even if someone copies a link, it expires.
  • Only members of the conversation can generate a signed URL.
  • Files are automatically deleted 24 hours after upload to keep JustGo free for everyone.

Your account

  • No real email is required — your username is tied to an internal identifier.
  • You can update your display name, photo, and course any time from your profile.
  • Logging out removes your session from the browser immediately.

Once you sign up, your classmates can find you by searching your username, display name, or your course & year. This means every student in every batch and stream is reachable in one place.

📖 How to use it

  1. Sign up with a username, display name, and your course & year.
  2. Search for a classmate by name or course — or create a group for your study team.
  3. Start chatting. Send text, files, replies, and reactions.
  4. Post a status to let everyone know what you're working on.
  5. Download files you need before they auto-delete in 24 hours.
  6. Stay connected with browser notifications for new messages.
